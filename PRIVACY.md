# Tetra Mix Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026
**Publisher:** TetraMos Technologies, Lagos, Nigeria
**Contact:** tetramostechnologies@gmail.com

Tetra Mix is a live video production app for Windows. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **your audio, video and settings stay on your PC unless you choose to send them somewhere** (for example, by going live).

## What we do not do

- No user accounts or sign-up.
- No analytics, tracking or usage statistics.
- No advertising.
- We do not sell or share personal information. TetraMos Technologies does not receive any data from the app.

## Camera and microphone

Tetra Mix uses the cameras and microphones you add as inputs.

- **Speech recognition** (used to cue scripture and follow song lyrics) runs entirely on your PC. Audio is processed in memory and is not saved or sent anywhere by this feature.
- Camera pictures and microphone sound leave your PC **only** when you stream, record to a location you choose, or send them out over your network (for example, NDI® output).

## Information stored on your PC

The app saves its own data on your PC, including settings, layouts, song libraries, running orders and recordings. Streaming keys are stored encrypted with Windows' own data protection, tied to your Windows account. Logs and crash reports are written to your PC only and are not uploaded.

## Network connections

The app connects to the internet or your local network only for these purposes:

| Purpose | When | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| Live streaming | When you go live | The platforms you choose (YouTube, Facebook, etc.) receive your stream under their own privacy policies |
| Upload speed test | When you run the test before going live | Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com) receives test data and, like any website, your IP address |
| Streaming server list | When the app starts | The OBS Project (obsproject.com) - the app downloads an updated list of streaming servers; no personal information is sent beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address) |
| Network and phone inputs | When you add them | The devices you connect (phones, NDI® sources, network cameras) |
| Web content in titles or browser inputs | When you add a web address | The website you entered |
| Remote control page | While the app is running | Devices on your local network that open the page |

## Feedback and support

If you report a problem on GitHub, GitHub's privacy policy applies to what you post there, and your post is public. If you email us, we use your email only to reply. If you donate by bank transfer, we see what any bank statement shows - the sender's name, the amount and any reference you add - and use it only to keep our records and to thank you. If you donate through a payment service, that service handles your payment details; we do not see your card details.

## Children

Tetra Mix is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
